# Contact card-fan image prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the cinematic Contact page social CTA.

- **Florida Keys:** aerial view of a small boat travelling through brilliant turquoise channels between mangrove islands, with coral kayaks on deck.
- **Miami colour:** a family cycling safely past pastel Art Deco buildings and palms on colourful bicycles in golden morning light.
- **Wild springs:** a split-level view of a family discovering gentle manatees beneath a crystal-clear Florida spring from a boardwalk.
- **Gulf sunsets:** a family enjoying a colourful sunset picnic on a quiet Gulf Coast beach.

All images were requested as photorealistic vertical editorial panels with vivid Florida colour and without readable text, logos or watermarks.

The Miami cycling image was subsequently edited with the built-in image generation tool. Only the adult woman on the mint-green bicycle was changed: her tied crop top was replaced with an opaque, full-length light-blue short-sleeve shirt. Her identity, pose, bicycle, shorts, family, buildings, lighting and composition were kept unchanged.
